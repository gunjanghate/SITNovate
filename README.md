# 🚀 SupaPrep

**Revolutionizing Skilling & Career Acceleration with AI**

SupaPrep is an all-in-one platform designed to streamline recruitment, skill development, and interview preparation using cutting-edge AI tools. Whether you're a job seeker, student, or recruiter, SupaPrep is your smart assistant for navigating the modern career landscape.

## 🌟 Features

### 🔎 Realtime Job & Internship Finder
A platform that aggregates job and internship listings from various sources, allowing users to:
- Search and filter listings based on preferences.
- Receive real-time updates.
- Stay ahead in their job hunt.

### 🧠 AI-Driven Interview Preparation
Prepare smarter, not harder:
- Personalized feedback on mock interviews.
- Skill gap analysis.
- Progress tracking and strategic alignment.

### 📄 Resume Shortlisting
Let AI handle the heavy lifting:
- Automatically curate resumes.
- Match top talent with organizational needs efficiently.

### 🤖 AI-Driven WhatsApp Bot
An intelligent chatbot that automates WhatsApp conversations using AI & NLP:
- Career guidance.
- Instant FAQs.
- Scheduling support.

### 🗣️ Mentor Chat
Connect with industrial experts via an AI-powered mentor chatbot to:
- Get answers to your career-related queries.
- Receive tailored advice.
